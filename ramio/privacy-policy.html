Privacy policy — Ramio

Last updated: 16 July 2026

This is an English translation of the French policy; in case of discrepancy, the French version prevails.

This policy describes the data processed by the “Ramio” mobile app (a manipulation rummy card game, hereafter “the app”) and the players’ rights.

Who is responsible for the data?

The app is published by Tristan Hurel. Contact: ramio.contact@gmail.com

What data is collected?

The app collects the strict minimum needed for the game to work:

  • E-mail address and password: to create the account and sign in. The password is never stored in clear text (it is hashed by our authentication provider). If you choose Sign in with Google or Apple, the app only receives the e-mail address of the chosen account (no access to your other Google or Apple data).
  • Guest account: you can try the game without creating an account. An anonymous technical account (with no e-mail address) is then created to keep your progress; you can convert it into a full account at any time, or delete it like any other account.
  • Nickname: the name shown to other players during a game. It is unique and visible to signed-in players (friends are added by nickname).
  • Friends list and invitations: the friendships you create in the game and the room invitations exchanged between friends.
  • Messages between friends: the private messages you exchange with your friends from the “My friends” screen are stored so that the conversation remains readable. They are visible only to you and your correspondent, and are deleted with the account.
  • Game results: score, ranking, date and game mode of every finished game, to feed the “My profile” screen (personal statistics, history and Elo ranking).
  • Notification token: a technical device identifier provided by the notification service, used to receive friend invitations as push notifications. Deleted at sign-out.
  • Reports: if you report a player (nickname or inappropriate remarks), we keep the reported nickname, the reason you give and the date, in order to moderate the game. Room chat messages are never stored; offensive words are masked automatically.
  • Blocked players: if you block a player, we keep that link (your account, the blocked account, the date) to prevent that player from contacting you (friend requests, messages, invitations, in-game chat). The blocked player is not informed; you can unblock them at any time from the “My friends” screen. These links are deleted with the account.
  • Technical connection data: IP address and technical logs, processed transiently to route online games and secure the service.
  • Anonymous audience measurement: to know whether the game is enjoyed and to improve it, the app sends a random device identifier (never linked to your account or e-mail address), the date of first opening, the days of use, two discovery milestones (tutorial finished, first game started) and the visual theme (“kit”) chosen in the app. These measurements cannot identify you.
  • Crash reports: in case of a technical error, an anonymous report (device model, system and app versions, technical trace of the error) is sent to our provider Sentry (ingested in the European Union) to fix bugs. It is not linked to your account.

The app collects no location data, no photos, and contains no advertising and no advertising tracker.

Phone contacts (optional feature)

The app offers to find your friends among your contacts. This feature never activates on its own: it asks for your consent in the app, then for the system permission.

If you use it:

  • only encrypted fingerprints (SHA-256 hashes) of your contacts’ e-mail addresses are sent to our servers — never the names, never the addresses in clear text, never the phone numbers;
  • these fingerprints are compared on the fly with those of existing accounts to suggest matching players, then forgotten: nothing from your address book is kept;
  • you can refuse or revoke the permission at any time in the phone settings, without losing any other feature of the game.

Where is the data stored?

  • Accounts and game results are stored at Supabase (database hosting provider), in a data centre located in the European Union.
  • The game server (real-time games) is hosted at Fly.io; the state of a game in progress is kept in memory only, for the duration of the game.

Exchanges between the app and the servers are encrypted (HTTPS/WSS).

How long is the data kept?

  • Account and game history: as long as the account exists.
  • When the account is deleted, the associated personal data is deleted or anonymised without undue delay.

Who has access to the data?

Nobody other than the publisher and its technical providers (Supabase, Fly.io, Google Firebase for delivering notifications, and Sentry for crash reports), strictly to the extent needed to run the service. The data is neither sold, nor rented, nor shared for advertising or commercial purposes. The other players in a game only see your nickname and your game actions.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data.

You can delete your account directly in the app: “My profile” screen, “Delete my account” button (see the Delete your account page). Deletion is immediate and erases the account, the statistics and the game history.

To exercise your other rights, or to request deletion without access to the app, write to ramio.contact@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

Minors

The app is a card game with no money at stake, no in-app purchase to date and no inappropriate content. Creating an account requires a valid e-mail address.

Changes

This policy may evolve (for instance when new features are added). The version in force is published at the address given on the App Store and Google Play listings, with its update date.
